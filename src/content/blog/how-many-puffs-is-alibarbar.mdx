## How long does each one last?

A rated puff is a lab figure: a standard short puff, repeated until the liquid runs out. Longer pulls use more liquid, so treat these as estimates.

| Model | 100 puffs a day | 200 puffs a day | 300 puffs a day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pandora (7,000) | About 70 days | About 35 days | About 23 days |
| Ingot (9,000) | About 90 days | About 45 days | About 30 days |
| Upload (25,000) | About 8 months | About 4 months | About 12 weeks |

Heat matters too. Alibarbar says "extreme temperatures can influence battery efficiency, e-liquid viscosity and vapor production" and recommends storing devices "in cool, dry conditions". In an Australian summer, that means not in the car.

## Pandora, Ingot or Upload: which should you get?

- **Pandora** if you want to try flavours without committing. It's the cheapest up front, and Alibarbar makes it in 18 flavours plus a "flavour roulette".
- **Ingot** if you want a disposable that lasts over a month. Alibarbar's page says it has "a convenient LED display on the side to monitor real-time battery and liquid levels", so you can see the end coming, and it comes in 25 flavours.
- **Upload** if you vape every day and would rather replace your vape a few times a year. It charges over USB-C and has more than one mode; Alibarbar says the "motor will vibrate once when change the mode".

We stock 13 Pandora, 17 Ingot and 14 Upload flavours. Browse them all in our [Alibarbar range](/product-category/alibarbar).
