IGET and Alibarbar are the two disposable brands Australians ask us about most, and the honest answer to "which is better?" is that they're good at different things. IGET has the bigger range and the classic icy-fruit flavours most people start on. Alibarbar wins on puff count and price per puff, especially with its 25,000-puff Upload. Here's how they compare on the numbers, using our own range and prices.

<ShortAnswer>

- **Choose IGET** for flavour choice (120 flavours in our range against Alibarbar's 26), the icy fruit profile, and models from a compact 3,500-puff bar to the 12,000-puff IGET One.
- **Choose Alibarbar** for the most puffs for your money: the Upload is rated at 25,000 puffs for $49 to $59, about $2 per 1,000 puffs.
- **Can't decide?** Start with one of each. Mixing brands in one order is fine.

## Flavours: icy fruit or sweet and candy

This is where most people end up choosing. Across our range, IGET makes up 182 single disposables in 120 different flavours. Alibarbar has 60 in 26 flavours.

- **IGET leans icy and fruity:** Grape Peach Ice, Blackberry Ice, Strawberry Watermelon Ice and Watermelon Mint Ice are typical. If you like a cold finish, IGET has more ways to get it.
- **Alibarbar leans sweet:** Mango Magic, Pink Lemon, Blueberry Breeze, Rainbow Candy and a run of lolly-shop flavours like Chupa Chups Strawberry. If you have a sweet tooth, start here.

Flavour is personal, so the cheapest way to find yours is a single device before committing to a bulk box.

## Design and features

- **Screens.** The IGET One and the Alibarbar Upload both have a display showing how much battery and e-liquid are left, so you're never caught out by an empty device.
- **Refills.** The [IGET Bar Plus](/product-category/iget-bar-plus) and the Alibarbar Upload both take replacement pods, so you keep the battery and swap the liquid. With a single-use device, the whole thing goes once it's empty.
- **Size.** The IGET Bar is the most pocketable of the lot. The high-puff models (IGET One, Alibarbar Upload) are chunkier because they carry bigger batteries and more liquid.

## Which one should you buy?

1. **New to disposables, or only vape now and then:** an [IGET Bar](/product-category/iget-bar-australia). Low upfront cost, compact, and the flavour range to find what you like.
2. **You like icy fruit flavours:** IGET, any size. The [Bar Plus](/product-category/iget-bar-plus) is the best value once you're buying pods.
3. **You want the lowest cost per puff:** the [Alibarbar Upload](/product-category/alibarbar-upload-25000-puffs). Nothing in either range comes close.
4. **You want one device that lasts about two months:** the [IGET One](/product-category/iget-one-12000) (12,000 puffs) or, for longer still, the Upload.
5. **You like sweet, candy-style flavours:** the [Alibarbar Pandora](/product-category/alibarbar-pandora) is the easy way in, at the same price as an IGET Bar Plus.
