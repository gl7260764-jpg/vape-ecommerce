## Five ways to make a pod last longer

1. **Take shorter puffs.** It's the biggest single factor. Two seconds instead of four can come close to doubling the puffs you get from a pod.
2. **Keep it out of the heat.** A car dashboard in a Brisbane or Perth summer is the fastest way to ruin a vape. Heat thins the liquid, which can leak or flood the coil, and it's hard on the battery. Keep it in a bag or pocket instead.
3. **Charge before it's completely flat.** A nearly flat battery gives thin, weak hits, and most people make up for it with longer pulls, which burns through liquid. Plug it in when you notice the vapour thinning.
4. **Store it upright.** It helps keep liquid in the pod and away from the mouthpiece. A drawer or the side pocket of a bag is fine.
5. **Don't chain-vape.** Several puffs back to back don't give the coil time to soak up fresh liquid, which leads to dry, burnt hits that cut a pod's life short.

> Two-second puffs, kept out of a hot car, are the cheapest way to get more out of every pod.

## Signs your pod is finished

- **The flavour fades or tastes burnt, even right after charging.** That's an empty pod. Swap it.
- **The vapour thins and then picks up again after a charge.** That was the battery, not the pod.
- **The light flashes when you take a puff.** On most rechargeable disposables that means a flat battery. Charge it and try again before you swap anything.

If a fresh pod still tastes burnt on a fully charged battery, the pod may not be seated properly. Take it out, check the contacts are clean and dry, and click it back in.
