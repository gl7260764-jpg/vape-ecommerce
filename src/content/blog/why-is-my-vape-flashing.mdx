On the vapes whose makers explain it, a flashing light means one of three things: the battery is low, it's charging, or it has cut out to protect itself. A burnt taste usually means the coil is heating with too little liquid to soak it, and a hiss is either the normal sound of a puff or, with heat, a smell or swelling, a battery to stop using straight away. Here's how to tell which, model by model where the makers say, and what to do about each.

<ShortAnswer>

- **Flashing:** usually a **low battery** (charge it) or **charging** in progress. Some models also flash after a puff held too long, or when the child lock is on.
- **Burnt taste:** the liquid is running out, or the wick hasn't had time to soak. Swap the pod, or retire the disposable.
- **Hissing with heat, smell or swelling:** stop using it and unplug it. Fire Rescue Victoria lists hissing among the signs of a failing battery.

</ShortAnswer>

Alibarbar's newer devices use a screen rather than a blinking light: the [Ingot](/product-category/alibarbar-ingot) has an LED display that shows "real-time battery and liquid levels", according to [Alibarbar](https://www.alibarbar.com/vape/ingot).

If your model isn't listed, work through the likely causes in order:

1. **Charge it.** Low battery is what the flashing means on the IGET models above that explain it. Plug it in and try again once it has had some charge.
2. **Take a shorter puff.** Some devices stop a puff that goes on too long, like the E-Hookah at 12 seconds, so a flash right after a very long drag may be that.
3. **Check the child lock.** Some IGETs have one. On the [IGET Bar Plus](/product-category/iget-bar-plus) V4, IGET says you turn it on or off by pressing the button "5 times within 2 seconds".
4. **Reseat the pod.** On pod devices like the Bar Plus, take the pod out, check the contacts are clean and dry, and click it back in.

If it still flashes on a full charge with a seated pod, the device or pod has failed. Contact whoever sold it to you.

## Why does my vape taste burnt?

A vape heats a small coil against a wick that soaks up e-liquid. While the wick is wet you get vapour; when it runs dry, the coil heats the wick itself and you taste it. That's why a burnt taste usually means one of these:

| When it tastes burnt | Most likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Near the end of a pod or device | The liquid is nearly gone | Swap the pod, or replace the disposable |
| After several puffs in a row | The wick hasn't had time to soak up more liquid | Wait a minute between sessions |
| On long, hard pulls | More liquid is used than the wick can bring up | Take shorter, gentler puffs |
| From the first puff of a new one | A faulty device, or not the real thing | [Check the code on the box](/blog/how-to-tell-if-iget-is-fake), then contact the seller |

Charging won't fix a burnt taste caused by an empty pod. If it still tastes burnt right after a full charge, the liquid is the problem, not the battery. For the Bar Plus, see [the signs a pod is finished](/blog/how-long-does-an-iget-bar-plus-last).

## Why is my vape hissing?

Two very different sounds get called hissing.

**A soft crackle or sizzle while you inhale** is the coil turning liquid into vapour. It's normal, and it stops when you stop puffing.

**A hiss when you're not using it**, or one that comes with heat, a smell or a change in shape, is a warning. [Fire Rescue Victoria](https://www.frv.vic.gov.au/battery-safety-consumers) says to "stop using the battery if you notice strong odours, colour change, extreme heat, change in shape, … leaking or unusual sounds (for example popping or hissing)". Unplug it if it's charging, leave it on a hard surface away from anything that burns, and don't use it again. If it catches fire, call 000.

## Charging it safely

Every rechargeable vape has a lithium-ion battery, and Australia's safety regulators give the same advice for all of them:

- **Use the right charger.** "Only use chargers and cords that are originally supplied with that device," says [Fire Rescue Victoria](https://www.frv.vic.gov.au/battery-safety-consumers). The [ACCC](https://www.productsafety.gov.au/consumers/handle-chemicals-and-poisons-safely/lithium-ion-batteries-guide) adds "or a reputable brand recommended by the manufacturer".
- **Unplug it when it's full.** Once the light says it's charged, take it off the charger.
- **Charge on a hard surface.** The ACCC says "never charge lithium-ion batteries or products on flammable materials such as beds, sofas or carpet".
- **Keep it out of a hot car.** "Don't leave batteries or vape products in your car, particularly on hot days," warns [WA's Building and Energy](https://www.wa.gov.au/government/announcements/battery-burn-warning-after-pocket-fires-flares-and-fireworks). Alibarbar notes that extreme temperatures affect battery efficiency and vapour too.
- **Stop at the first sign of damage.** The ACCC says never use a battery that shows "signs of failure", like overheating, swelling, leaking or venting gas.
