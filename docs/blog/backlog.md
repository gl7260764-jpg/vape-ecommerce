*Researched 6–7 November 2026 from Google's Australian autocomplete (`hl=en-AU&gl=au`) and the store's own catalog.*

## The strategy

Broad phrases ("vapes australia", "disposable vapes online") belong to the big stores and the health departments.
We go after the **specific questions** people type, which nobody answers well yet: the current results for
"how long does an iget bar last" and "iget or alibarbar which is better" are spam pages on university sites,
with no real store answering. Each post owns one cluster of phrases that mean the same thing, and links to the
products and categories it talks about.

Local terms come in three ways, not by stuffing city names into posts:

- **State and territory**: "are vapes legal in qld", "vape laws nsw", "cannabis laws act". One post per state.
- **Australian situations**: Bali trips, flying domestically, summer heat in a parked car, regional delivery.
- **Australian prices and brands**: AUD prices, the ranges sold here (IGET, Alibarbar, HQD, Geek Bar).
